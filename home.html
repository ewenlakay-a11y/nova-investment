Customer questions

Can I withdraw immediately?

No. A locked investment is not normally withdrawable until maturity. Contact Customer Care if you need to request an early withdrawal.

What is the early withdrawal fee?

An approved early withdrawal has a 2% fee calculated from the investment value.

Can I use the same email twice?

No. Each email address can belong to only one account.
